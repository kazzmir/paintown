still stops and channel1 continues until its ordinary expiry. With direct

not cause contact's channel0 interruption. Ordinary deaths emit the original
voice and measured echoes. Restoring dead life to20 retains Alive0; restoring
a protected actor retains Alive1. The native snapshots also distinguish the
contact state5000, direct unprotected state5030 and protected state700.
